Check-in & Check-out

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in time is 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM. Check-out time is 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM.

If you need an early check-in or a late check-out, please reach out to us in advance via WhatsApp and we will try our best to accommodate you.

How does check-in work?

We offer a private, self-guided check-in experience. You'll receive detailed instructions and a property walkthrough before your arrival so you're all set from the moment you step in.

A valid government-issued photo ID is required from all adult guests at check-in.

Can I arrive late at night?

Please inform us in advance if you plan to arrive outside the standard check-in window. We will make suitable arrangements to ensure you have a smooth and comfortable arrival.

Amenities & Spaces

Is the entire home exclusively mine when I book?

Yes — completely. When you book Kodai Countryside Homestay, you get the entire 2-bedroom bungalow exclusively. Both bedrooms, the living room, dining area, kitchen, garden, and gazebo are all yours. No other guests share the property.

How many bedrooms and bathrooms does the property have?

The property has 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. Bedroom 1 (the Garden Room) has a double bed and a single bed. Bedroom 2 (the Hill Room) has a double bed. Both bathrooms are for the exclusive use of your group.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available throughout the property. Please note that Kodaikanal is a hill station and connectivity may occasionally vary depending on weather conditions.

Is parking available?

Yes, free off-street parking is available on the property. Bicycle parking is also available for guests who cycle.

Can I use the kitchen?

Yes, the fully equipped shared kitchen is available for your use. It is stocked with the essentials — gas stove, refrigerator, and cookware. You are welcome to prepare your own meals.

Is there an outdoor area?

Absolutely. The property features a beautifully landscaped garden, a shaded gazebo with seating, a balcony, an outdoor fireplace, and a BBQ grill — all with views of the surrounding hills. It's one of the best parts of staying here.

Policies & Rules

Are pets allowed?

Yes, 1 pet is allowed with prior approval. Please mention that you'll be bringing a pet when making your enquiry so we can make the necessary arrangements. The garden is great for animals!

Is smoking allowed on the property?

Smoking is not permitted inside the home. Guests who wish to smoke must do so in designated outdoor areas only.

Can I host a party or event?

Parties and organised events are not permitted on the property. This is a peaceful family home and we ask all guests to respect the nature of the space and the neighbourhood.

Are there quiet hours?

Yes. Quiet hours are observed from 11:00 PM to 5:00 AM. We ask guests to keep noise to a minimum during this time out of respect for the neighbours and the peaceful environment.

Can children stay?

Children of all ages are warmly welcome! The garden and outdoor spaces are perfect for families. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian throughout their stay.

Pricing & Booking

How much does it cost per night?

We do not list fixed rates online as pricing varies based on the season, number of guests, and duration of your stay. Please reach out to us via WhatsApp or the contact form for a personalised quote.

How do I book the property?

The easiest way is to WhatsApp us directly at +91 99623 55660 with your preferred dates, number of guests, and any special requirements. You can also use the contact form.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please contact us to discuss the cancellation terms applicable to your booking. We try to be fair and flexible — especially for unforeseen circumstances. We'll always communicate clearly before confirming your stay.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI transfers and bank transfers. Details will be shared at the time of booking confirmation. Please reach out to us if you have any specific requirements.

Location & Getting Here

Where exactly is the property located?

The homestay is located in Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu — 624101. Exact directions will be shared with you upon booking confirmation via WhatsApp.

What attractions are nearby?

Several popular Kodaikanal attractions are close by:

  • Kodaikanal Lake — 5 miles
  • Bear Shola Falls — 5 miles
  • Coaker's Walk — 5 miles
  • Bryant Park — nearby
  • Silver Cascade Falls — nearby
  • Madurai Airport — 85 miles
How do I get to Kodaikanal?

By air: The nearest airport is Madurai International Airport (IXM), approximately 85 miles away. From there, cabs are available.

By train: The nearest railway station is Kodaikanal Road (KQN), about 70 km from the hill station. Cabs and buses connect to Kodaikanal from there.

By road: Kodaikanal is well-connected by road from Madurai, Coimbatore, Bengaluru, and Chennai. State buses and private taxis are available.

Legal & Safety

Is ID required at check-in?

Yes, a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar card, Passport, Driving Licence, or Voter ID) is required from all adult guests at check-in as per local regulations. Please carry an original copy.

Is the property secure?

Yes. The property has 24-hour security and is well-maintained and secured. Kodaikanal is a safe hill town and the neighbourhood is quiet and residential.

What should I do in an emergency?

Contact us immediately on WhatsApp at +91 99623 55660. Emergency contact numbers (hospital, police) for Kodaikanal will also be provided in your check-in guide.

Rules & Regulations

Dear guests, to ensure the safety and comfort of all guests during their stay, we ask you to kindly follow these guidelines.

Check-in and Check-out times
  • Check-in is from 11:00 AM.
  • Check-out is until 10:00 AM.
Pets policy
  • Pets are allowed only in the living room.
  • Pets are not allowed on sofas or beds to maintain cleanliness.
  • Any damages to the property caused by pets will be charged accordingly.
Keys & access
  • Keys are provided by the caretaker at check-in. Guests are kindly asked to lock the rooms when not in use.
  • Keys must be handed back to the caretaker upon departure.
  • Guests who lose or do not return keys are obligated to pay the applicable penalty.
Property care & liability
  • The homestay bears no responsibility for any damage or loss of personal belongings. In case of any loss, please inform the caretaker immediately.
  • Any damages to the homestay property will be borne by the guest.
  • Room inventory items (pillows, blankets, towels) must not be removed from the property.
  • It is not permitted to bring explosives, weapons, inflammable materials, or dangerous chemicals onto the premises.
Smoking, Wi-Fi & services
  • The homestay is entirely non-smoking. Smoking is permitted only outside the building.
  • Wi-Fi internet is free and available throughout the property. The password is provided at check-in.
  • Food can be arranged on request through the caretaker. Additional charges apply.
  • Additional charges apply for kitchen utensil cleaning and room service.
Guest responsibility & feedback

By checking in, guests confirm that they have read and understood all homestay rules.

If you have any suggestions about our service, or if you encounter any technical issue in your room, please inform us at your earliest convenience. We are here to help.

We wish you a pleasant stay!
