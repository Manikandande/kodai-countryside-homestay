How it began

A Home That Became a Homestay

Kodai Countryside Homestay is a private family bungalow nestled in the Palani Hills of Kodaikanal — a place where the mist rolls in each morning and the garden blooms in quiet colours. The house was built to be lived in: warm interiors, large windows that frame the hills, a kitchen at the heart of it all.

We opened the home to guests because we believe a real place — with its own character, its own garden, its own silence — offers something a hotel simply cannot. When you stay here, you get the entire bungalow. Just your family, your pace, your holiday.

Most guests find us by word of mouth. Many come back. That, more than anything, tells us we're doing something right.

How we live here

Our Ethos

These aren't policies — they're the way our family has always operated.

🌿

Locally Sourced

Our vegetables, fruits, and dairy come from farms within 10 kilometres. We support local farmers and keep our footprint small.

🪵

Low Impact Living

We collect rainwater, compost our kitchen waste, and use solar water heating. Guests are welcome to see how the systems work.

🤫

Quiet by Design

We limit the number of guests at any time. There are no DJ nights or packaged group tours here — this is a place of genuine rest.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Genuinely Personal

We know every guest by name before they arrive and we're usually available to help with local directions, recommendations, or just conversation.

🌱

Children & Nature

We believe children should have unstructured time in nature. The grounds are safe, the surrounding trails are gentle, and the wildlife is abundant.

🔒

Your Privacy

We never photograph guests without permission, we don't share your personal details, and we don't pressure for reviews or social posts.
