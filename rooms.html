The Property

A private 2-bedroom family bungalow nestled in the Palani Hills — with a garden, gazebo, full kitchen, and room enough to truly unwind.

What you're getting

The Whole Home. Only You.

When you book Kodai Countryside Homestay, you get the entire bungalow — not just a room. Two bedrooms, two bathrooms, a living room, dining area, fully equipped shared kitchen, and a lush garden with a gazebo. No other guests. No shared corridors. Just your family, your pace, your holiday.

Bedroom 1

The Garden Room

A serene, generously sized room with traditional carved wooden furniture and a calming green palette. Fitted with a double bed and a single bed — perfect for a couple or a small family. Natural light pours in through large windows overlooking the garden.

Double + Single Bed Carved Wooden Furniture Garden View Built-in Storage Natural Light

Bedroom 2

The Hill Room

A bold, beautiful room dressed in deep blue with generous overhead cabinets and a double bed. The windows let in cool mountain air and soft light throughout the day. A great sleeping room — restful, quiet, and comfortable for a couple.

Double Bed Built-in Overhead Cabinets Blue Accent Walls Mountain Air Wardrobe

Common Space

The Lounge

A warm, welcoming living room centred around a beautifully carved wooden sofa set — the heart of the home. Curl up with a book, gather the family for a movie, or simply sit and enjoy the cool Kodaikanal evenings. The TV lounge area is right next door.

Carved Wooden Sofa Set TV Lounge Family Gathering Space Warm Ambiance

Dining & Kitchen

Cook. Eat. Linger.

A proper dining area with a glass-top table and wooden chairs — just right for a family breakfast or a candlelit dinner. Adjacent is the fully equipped shared kitchen with wooden cabinets, a gas stove, and all the essentials you need to cook your favourite meals.

Step outside and breathe. A beautifully maintained garden wraps around the bungalow, bursting with flowers and greenery. Sit under the gazebo with your morning chai, light the outdoor fireplace in the evening, and enjoy a BBQ under the stars. The mountains are always in view.

Good to know

House Policies

Check-in

Check-in time is 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM. We offer private, self-guided check-in for your convenience. Please let us know your arrival time in advance.

Check-out

Check-out time is 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM. Early or late arrangements can be discussed — just reach out to us ahead of your stay.

No Smoking

Smoking is strictly not permitted inside the home. Guests who wish to smoke may do so in designated outdoor areas only.

No Parties or Events

Parties, events, and large gatherings are not permitted on the property. This is a family home and a peaceful retreat for all guests.

Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are observed from 11:00 PM to 5:00 AM out of respect for the neighbourhood and the natural environment.

Pets

1 pet is allowed with prior approval. Pets are welcome in the living room and garden but not on sofas or beds. Any property damage caused by pets will be charged accordingly.

ID Required

A valid government-issued photo ID is required from all adult guests at check-in as per local regulations.

Children & Minors

Children of all ages are warmly welcome. Guests under 18 years must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian throughout their stay.

Keys

Keys are provided by the caretaker at check-in. Please lock the home when stepping out. Keys must be returned upon departure — lost keys incur a replacement charge.

Food & Kitchen

Food can be arranged on request through the caretaker (charges apply). The kitchen is available for self-catering. Additional charges apply for kitchen utensil cleaning and room service.
